Pan-India Shipping & Delivery Policy

Reliable, damage-free transit for your precious everlasting floral creations.

1. Free Shipping Threshold (₹999)

Floreal provides FREE standard express delivery on all orders within India with a cart subtotal of ₹999.00 or higher. For orders under ₹999, a flat subsidised delivery fee of ₹99.00 is automatically applied at checkout.

2. Handcrafting & Dispatch Lead Times

Because every single petal and leaf is hand-knitted by skilled women artisans, orders require a brief crafting window before dispatch:

  • Ready-to-Ship Best Sellers (Single Rose, Tulips, Desk Pots): Dispatched within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Custom Bouquets & Luxury Hampers: Handcrafted and dispatched within 2 to 4 business days.

4. Reinforced Crush-Proof Packaging Guarantee

Unlike natural flowers which wilt or delicate glass gifts that shatter, our crochet flowers are naturally durable. To ensure your bouquet arrives in photo-ready perfection:

  • Every arrangement is enveloped in protective tissue paper.
  • The blooms are anchored inside a 5-ply reinforced corrugated craft box designed to withstand heavy transit impact.
  • Greeting cards and fragrance sachets are enclosed in a sealed protective sleeve.
