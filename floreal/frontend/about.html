The Problem with Natural Flowers

Think back to the last bouquet of fresh flowers you received or gifted for a birthday, anniversary, or Valentine's Day. It arrived vibrant, fragrant, and breathtaking. Yet within 48 to 72 hours, the petals curled, brown edges appeared, the water grew murky, and the bouquet had to be discarded.

At Floreal, we asked a simple question: Why should the most meaningful gestures of love and celebration come with an expiration date?

Enter the Art of Forever Crochet

Floreal was born out of deep reverence for traditional hand-knitting and crochet artistry. Instead of fast-plastic artificial plants or ephemeral cut flowers, we turn 100% premium milk cotton and plush chenille yarn into everlasting botanical sculptures.

Each rose petal, sunflower center, and lily leaf is crocheted stitch-by-stitch by hand. A single signature bouquet takes our master artisans anywhere from 3 to 8 hours of dedicated handwork. When you touch a Floreal bloom, you feel the soft, warm texture of yarn — tactile, calming, and eternal.

Our Core Social Mission: Women Artisan Empowerment

Over 90% of Floreal’s creations are hand-knitted by homemakers and skilled women artisans in semi-urban and rural communities across India. By offering flexible home-based livelihood opportunities, fair wages, and creative recognition, every purchase directly supports women financial independence.

The Shark Tank India Milestone

In 2026, Floreal stepped onto the national stage on Shark Tank India Season 5. Presenting our vision of sustainable, everlasting gifting to the Sharks validated that consumers across India are eager for emotional, permanent keepsakes that honor both craftsmanship and environmental mindfulness.
