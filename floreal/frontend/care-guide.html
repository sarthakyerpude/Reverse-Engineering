The Forever Flower Care Guide

Your crochet blooms require zero watering and minimal upkeep to stay fresh for years.

🚫💧

Rule #1: Never Water

Crochet yarn blooms don’t need moisture! Keep away from open water or humid rain.

✨🧹

Rule #2: Gentle Dusting

Once every few weeks, gently brush petals with a soft makeup brush or cool blow dryer.

1. How to Reshape Stems & Petals

Every flower stem is built with flexible, paper-wrapped floral wire. After unpacking, or when styling into a new vase:

  • Gently fan out the petals outward to restore maximum fullness.
  • Bend the green stem slightly to create a natural, organic floral curve.
  • If displaying in a short vase, you can safely bend the lower end of the wire stem upward rather than cutting it.

2. Adding Natural Floral Fragrance

Want your forever bouquet to smell like fresh roses, lavender, or jasmine?

You can lightly spritz your favorite perfume, room mist, or essential oil from a distance of 20–30 cm onto the green leaves or stem wrap. Cotton yarn naturally holds pleasant fragrances for days without staining!

3. Direct Sunlight

Our premium milk cotton yarn uses fade-resistant dyes. However, like any luxury textile or artwork, prolonged placement in direct harsh tropical midday sunlight over many months may cause subtle lightening. Displaying in ambient indoor light preserves peak vibrant colors indefinitely.
