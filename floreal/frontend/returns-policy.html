Returns & 100% Replacement Guarantee

Our commitment to peace of mind: handcrafting perfection delivered safe and sound.

1. Handcrafted Nature of Products

Because every Floreal forever bouquet is handmade with care by individual women artisans, minor variations in stitching tightness, yarn dye lot, and shape are natural signatures of authentic handcrafted artistry rather than defects. No two bouquets are identical!

2. 48-Hour Transit Damage Replacement Guarantee

If your bouquet, ceramic pot, or gift hamper arrives damaged due to rough courier handling, we offer a 100% FREE replacement without delay.

Important Requirement: Unboxing Video
To claim a transit damage replacement, please record a continuous, uncut video while unboxing your parcel from Floreal and send it to our WhatsApp team within 48 hours of delivery.

3. Simple 3-Step Replacement Process

  1. Record a quick unboxing video showing the shipping label and the damaged bloom.
  2. WhatsApp the video along with your Order ID (`#FLO-XXXXX`) to +91 98765 43210.
  3. Our artisan lead will verify the claim within 4 hours and dispatch a brand-new replacement bouquet via Express Courier at zero cost to you!

4. Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled within 6 hours of placement before the artisan begins assembly. Once an order enters the Crafting or Packed stage, customized items cannot be cancelled.
