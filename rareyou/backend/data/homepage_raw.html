Frequently Asked Questions

Are Rare You products completely handmade?

Yes. Every Rare You creation is carefully handcrafted by skilled women artisans using premium yarns and materials. Each piece takes hours of detailed work — no machines, no shortcuts — just pure craftsmanship and heart.

Why are Rare You products priced higher than other handicrafts?

Our prices reflect the time, quality, and artistry behind every piece. We use rare yarns that no other brand in India works with and follow a strict quality process for durability and finish. You’re not just buying a product — you’re owning a handcrafted piece of art made to last for years.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, Rare You products are loved worldwide. We proudly ship from India to many international locations. Shipping time and charges may vary depending on your country.

How long does delivery take?

Within India, deliveries usually take 5–8 business days. International orders may take 10–15 business days depending on location. Each piece is packed with care and shipped securely.

What is Partial COD?

Partial COD means you pay a small advance amount online to confirm your order, and the remaining balance in cash upon delivery. Since every Rare You piece is handcrafted with care, this helps us ensure genuine orders while giving you the convenience of paying most of the amount at delivery.

At Rare You, we craft more than products we craft memories, while bringing India’s rare art to the world.
